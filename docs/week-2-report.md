## Assumptions, out loud

- **Simulated traffic.** Visitors, questions and follow-ups are seeded scripts (175 visitors a day in `sim_assumptions.json`); the real volume and mix are not known. Every row is labelled simulated.
- **Seeded outcomes.** Control outcomes and every rating are drawn from rules in `sim_assumptions.json`, not measured from customers. Only the assistant's answers, hand-offs and cost are real model behaviour, so the rating line holds by construction.
- **Repeat contact is not measured.** Not measured: the old chats come from 15 customers, so they give no repeat-contact baseline.
- **Linear scale-up.** Hours and wrong answers at another share multiply the sample by 1 / share: the same mix, the same resolve rate. 4 minutes for a contact with a person is a flat assumption.
- **Tiny samples.** 96 assistant chats, 19 of them needing a person. Read the 95 % intervals, not only the observed values: a line that holds on its observed value is not proven below its limit unless its interval is.
- **Only order status is live.** 47 assistant-group chats on other questions went straight to a person and are not compared.
- **The scoring rules.** Each answer is scored against the carrier's file the service had loaded. A reply that says the news is old is not a status given as fact; a negated status is not a claim; a partly shipped order whose parcels are all delivered is right only if the reply names the part-shipped status. The decisions are in `docs/plan-golive.md`.
- **The night file hour.** 03:00 UTC for the carrier's night file is a working value until Ravi Menon's team confirms it (OQ-5 in `docs/requirements.md`).
